## What It Is
Age play is roleplay where consenting adults inhabit an age headspace different from their biological age. This may include younger or older headspaces. It involves adults only and never involves actual minors.

## Common Forms
- Little: childlike headspace, often paired with a caregiver dynamic.
- Middle: older child to teen headspace, often with more independence.
- Adult Baby: infant-style regression.
- Age Progression: playing older than biological age.

## Core Traits
- Finds genuine comfort or enjoyment in a different age headspace.
- May use regression for stress relief or emotional release.
- May experience the alternate age as a distinct but real part of self.
- Can be sexual, non-sexual, or mixed depending on the relationship.

## Practice Markers
- Age-coded activities, objects, or rituals.
- Shifts in vocabulary, speech, or emotional posture.
- Caregiver relationships or protective dynamics.
- Clothing, accessories, or comfort items tied to the headspace.

## Boundaries
- Adults only.
- Clear separation between fantasy and reality.
- Must be consensual and actively welcomed by both partners.
- Not everyone is comfortable with it; that is a valid incompatibility.

- Use only if the source character, route energy, or explicit direction strongly supports it.
- Do not assign it just to make the intimacy profile look unusual.
- If used, present it as a grounded adult dynamic, not shock value.
