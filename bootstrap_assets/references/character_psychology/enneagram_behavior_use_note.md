# Enneagram Behavior Use Note

## Purpose
This note distills the usable part of the Enneagram datasets currently reviewed for CharacterGen:
- `517 (Sample 1).xlsx`
- `278 (Sample 2).xlsx`
- `181 (Sample 3).xlsx`

Use it as a light behavioral reference layer, not as a scientific truth engine.

## Non-Verbatim Rule
Use this file to shape motivation and stress behavior, not to reproduce typology language directly.

If the output sounds like an Enneagram explainer instead of lived behavior, rewrite it into concrete character logic.

## What To Borrow

### Stress Style
Enneagram is most useful when it helps answer:
- what kind of pressure gets under this person's skin
- what they protect first
- what they do when they feel exposed, cornered, overlooked, or unloved

Translate type into:
- control habits
- emotional defense
- conflict posture
- shame triggers
- intimacy friction

### Repeating Motivation Patterns
The useful part is not the label itself but the recurring motive shape.

Examples:
- Type 1:
  - needs things to be right, clean, fair, or controlled
  - reacts strongly to sloppiness, moral failure, or self-loss
- Type 2:
  - wants to be needed, valued, and emotionally wanted
  - may over-give, anticipate needs, or hide hurt behind helpfulness
- Type 3:
  - manages image, competence, momentum, and visible value
  - may convert insecurity into performance
- Type 4:
  - guards identity, depth, meaning, and emotional distinctiveness
  - may intensify longing, shame, or private comparison
- Type 5:
  - protects energy, privacy, distance, and self-sufficiency
  - may retreat into observation, reserve, or controlled detachment
- Type 6:
  - scans for risk, loyalty, danger, and reliability
  - may test, doubt, brace, or seek certainty
- Type 7:
  - avoids internal confinement through motion, options, stimulation, or reframing
  - may dodge pain by speeding past it
- Type 8:
  - protects autonomy, strength, dignity, and control
  - may push first rather than risk being handled
- Type 9:
  - protects internal peace, continuity, and emotional equilibrium
  - may numb, soften, defer, or disappear around conflict

### Contradiction and Mask
Enneagram is good at helping shape:
- public mask vs private fear
- what the character refuses to admit
- how attraction and closeness threaten their control system

That makes it useful for:
- romance friction
- apology style
- conflict repair difficulty
- jealousy reactions
- tenderness under strain

## What Not To Overclaim
- do not treat Enneagram as hard science
- do not present it as diagnostic truth
- do not assume every person cleanly matches one type
- do not use type as a replacement for attachment style, trauma response, values, or social context
- do not turn a type label into a full personality by itself

The reviewed datasets are especially limited because:
- samples are modest in size
- at least one file is demographically narrow
- one variable map is partially sloppy or reused
- some correlations may reflect sample culture more than universal human structure

## How CharacterGen Should Use It

### Best Use
Use Enneagram as a secondary interpretive layer that helps answer:
- what this character defends most fiercely
- what kind of wound or shame they organize around
- how they distort connection under pressure
- what softens them
- what closeness costs them emotionally

### Not Best Use
Do not use it to:
- certify psychological accuracy
- force characters into rigid stereotypes
- generate type slogans instead of behavior
- pretend the model has run a real assessment

## Translation Rule
Never stop at:
- "she's a 4"
- "he's an 8"
- "they read like a 5"

Always continue into behavior:
- what they do when jealous
- how they speak when cornered
- what intimacy threatens
- what kind of reassurance lands
- what kind of attention feels invasive

## CharacterGen Takeaway
Enneagram is worth keeping in the system as a soft pattern language.

It is good for:
- motivation flavor
- defense style
- stress behavior
- attraction friction
- contradiction building

It is not good for:
- scientific validation claims
- standalone personality generation
- replacing broader grounded psychology
