# Beau-Style Athlete Route Scene Scaffold

## Purpose
This is a scene-engine scaffold distilled from the Beau best-friend route.

Use it when you want:
- athlete-route pressure
- public attention versus private claim
- ritual intimacy before formal confession
- a route that keeps moving through events instead of stalling in one room

## Non-Verbatim Rule
Use this scaffold as scene-engine inspiration, not a beat sheet to replay unchanged.

If the output starts reproducing this route event order too literally, abstract it into pressure, privacy shift, and label rupture rather than copying the stored sequence.

## Core Beat Chain

### 1. Public Inciting Contact
- crowded party or social circle
- forced intimacy game or public dare
- witnesses amplify embarrassment
- a third party is already hanging on him
- the contact itself is brief
- the aftermath matters more than the contact

### 2. Immediate Retreat And Pursuit
- she exits first
- he follows because the joke stopped being funny to him
- hallway, porch, stairwell, dorm doorway, parking lot edge
- the scene shifts from noisy/public to narrow/private very fast

### 3. Private Label Rupture
- an old “safe” label gets challenged
- banter stops protecting them
- one of them admits the label has been false, cowardly, or hurtful
- this is where the route becomes real

### 4. Unfinished Night
- they do not fully resolve everything
- one of them leaves keyed up, destabilized, or unable to sleep
- the emotional charge carries into the next day

### 5. Ritual Absence Beat
- a shared routine gets broken
- pregame stop
- morning coffee
- normal ride
- expected text
- familiar knock
- the missing ritual hurts more than a dramatic fight would

### 6. Public Duty Under Emotional Strain
- he has to perform while distracted
- game, race, event, stage, interview, team obligation
- she has to watch him be public while privately wrecked
- the route gains force because neither of them can fully stop the moment

### 7. Signal Across The Crowd
- they do not need a full conversation
- one look, one gesture, one private signal is enough
- this preserves intimacy under public conditions

### 8. Post-Performance Reunion
- parking lot
- truck tailgate
- tunnel exit
- locker room edge
- equipment room
- the public self is still physically present, but breaking down

### 9. Honesty Threshold
- the real confession lands here
- not polished
- not speechified
- usually tied to time, waiting, jealousy, or “I have wanted this longer than I admitted”

### 10. Possessive-Soft Landing
- after the heat breaks, the route should not jump straight into detached calm
- instead:
  - close body contact
  - practical caretaking
  - possessive language softened by reverence
  - water, shower, moving carefully, checking pain, staying close

## Pressure Sources To Keep Active
- teammates
- party friends
- the girl everyone assumes he should want
- coaches or event timing
- crowd noise
- public image
- rituals that can be missed, interrupted, or witnessed

## Texture Cues Worth Keeping
- cheap beer
- string lights
- scarred doorframes
- hoodie cuffs
- gravel
- tailgate metal
- sweat-dark hair
- grass stains
- stadium lights
- cramped bathroom steam
- detergent, skin salt, shampoo

## Relationship Movement Rule
This route works because each scene changes the pressure:
- public dare
- private confrontation
- delayed fallout
- missed ritual
- public performance
- private reunion
- intimate aftermath

Do not leave the pair circling in one emotional register for too long.
