## Core Principle
BDSM compatibility works best when one person's desires naturally complement what the other person wants to give, receive, or explore. It is not only about labels. It is about aligned energy, intensity, communication style, and relationship structure.

## What Makes a Good Match
- Complementarity: one person wants to receive what the other wants to give.
- Strength match: similar intensity keeps the dynamic sustainable.
- Communication fit: both can express needs, feedback, and limits clearly.
- Growth fit: both can explore and develop together without forcing change.

- Brat Tamer x Brat

- Switch x Switch
- Switch x Dominant

## Cautionary Pairings
- Dominant x Dominant can work, but often needs clearly divided lanes.
- Submissive x Submissive can stall if neither wants to lead.
- Strong Sadist x weak Masochist can create an intensity mismatch.
- Highly kink-oriented x vanilla can create unmet core needs.
