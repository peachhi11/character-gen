# Non-Monogamist

## Non-Verbatim Rule
Use this file to assess structure fit and compatibility, not to print stock non-monogamy descriptions.

If the output starts reading like an ethics glossary instead of a character-specific relationship structure, rewrite it.

## What It Is
Non-monogamy refers to ethical relationship structures that allow multiple romantic, sexual, or BDSM partners with honesty, consent, and communication from everyone involved.

## Common Forms
- Polyamory
- Open relationships
- Relationship anarchy
- Multiple play-partner arrangements

## Core Traits
- Comfortable with multiple meaningful connections.
- Requires strong communication and boundary clarity.
- Often values autonomy, honesty, and emotional complexity.
- May experience compersion or at least tolerate partner autonomy well.

## Practice Markers
- Explicit agreements about partners and boundaries.
- Ongoing conversations about jealousy, logistics, and health.
- Relationship structures that are intentionally negotiated rather than assumed.

## Compatibility Notes
- Best matched with people comfortable with ethical non-monogamy.
- Can be fundamentally incompatible with a clearly monogamous route or possessive pairing fantasy.

## Prompt Guidance
- Use only if the source character, setting, or explicit prompt direction clearly supports ethical non-monogamy.
- Do not assign it by default.
- Do not confuse it with fear of commitment or promiscuity.
- If the route energy is exclusivity, longing, possessiveness, or monogamous romance, prefer `None`.
